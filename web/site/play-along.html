Play along. It listens to every note.

Choose a song and start Play Along. Wait-for-me mode holds the music until you find each note; keep-the-beat mode plays the other parts in time. Afterwards you get a report: your score, the notes to fix, and the bars to loop.

Start playing, free

A clear report

A score out of 100, stars, a bar-by-bar strip, and a list like "bar 5: you played F, the score says F sharp".

Feel the mistakes

The Android app buzzes on a wrong note; on any screen the note shakes and the edge flashes red.

Record your take

Each take is recorded so you can listen back, or send it to your teacher with one tap.

Slow it down, loop it

Practise the hard bars at 60%, then speed up as you get them right.

Change the key

Move any song to a key that suits your voice or instrument.

Free library

4,900 free pieces to play, from Bach to folk songs, in staff notation and 簡譜.
