Questions

Good to know

Can I get the score of a current pop song?

Pop songs are protected by copyright, so we can't put them in the free library. Ask for the song on Stage: a musician writes an arrangement for you, and it is shared only with the people who asked for it. You can also import a score you already own.

How accurate is the AI reading of PDFs and photos?

On clean printed pages it reads nearly every note; dense piano music and phone photos have more slips. Every bar is checked, the bars it isn't sure of are marked, and the note editor fixes them quickly. That is why it is a Pro tool for people who want to edit and arrange, not a promise of a perfect copy.

Is MusicXML and MIDI import exact?

Yes. Those files are read on your device with exact timing, in a fraction of a second, and it is free.

Which devices does it work on?

Android (Google Play), iPhone and iPad (open the app in Safari and add it to your Home Screen), and any computer in the browser, where you can install it too. Your plan follows your account.

What are Notes?

Notes are what you earn on Stage for helping: making a requested score, a good answer, a daily challenge. They unlock useful things, like Pro days and AI page reads. They can't be bought or cashed out.

I teach music. How do I use Melovyn with my students?

Open Learn, choose "I teach" and sign in with Google. Make a class (private, or open to everyone), and send students the link or QR code. Assign songs; each student's Play Along results, wrong notes and recordings appear in your class, and you reply with comments and stickers. Music schools can run many classes the same way.

Can students find me as a private teacher?

Yes. Teachers set up a verified profile for a small one-time fee; students search for free, request a lesson, and you confirm the time. Lessons can be online or in person.

Does Play Along work on iPhone?

Yes, in Safari and as an app added to the Home Screen. Allow the microphone when asked. iPhone browsers cannot vibrate, so wrong notes flash red on the screen instead; the Android app also buzzes.
