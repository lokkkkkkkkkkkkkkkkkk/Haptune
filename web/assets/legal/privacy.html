ETASheet privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026. ETASheet is made by sloky.

The short version

Your music library, recordings and practice history stay on your device. We keep only what the community and your plan need: your profile, what you post, your credits and your practice minutes. We never sell data and there are no ads.

What stays on your device

  • Your pieces (scores, chord sheets, PDFs and photos you import) and your practice history.
  • The microphone: Play Along and the tuner listen on your device. A Play Along take is recorded on your device so you can listen back; it is sent only if you choose to send it to your teacher in a class, or post it on Stage.

What the ETASheet server keeps

  • Your account: a display name, username, avatar colour, instruments, level and short bio you choose; a password if you set one (stored only as a secure hash); the devices you signed in on. If you sign in with Google: your Google account ID and email address (the email is shown only to you), and your first name if you have not chosen one; nothing else from your Google account.
  • Community: requests, questions, answers, comments, scores and attachments you post; claps, follows, blocks and reports; notifications.
  • Credits and plan: your credit history, your plan and how many AI features you used this month.
  • Practice minutes per day, for streaks and leaderboards.
  • Classes: if you join a class, your results (scores, the bars and notes that went wrong, practice time) and the recordings you choose to send. Only the class teacher (and you) can see and listen to them. Open classes show their name, description, teacher's name and number of students to everyone.
  • Problem reports: what you write, and technical details (app version, device type, recent errors) only if you leave "Include technical details" on.

AI features

When you use Plus or Pro features (reading music from photos or PDFs, finding songs online, the Hapi tutor), the page, photo, search or question you send is processed by Anthropic's Claude API to produce the answer. It is not used to train AI models. Free features do not use AI.

Purchases

Subscriptions are bought through Google Play (and later the App Store). Payments are handled by the store; we never see your card. RevenueCat tells our server which plan your account has.

Who can see what

Your profile, public posts and leaderboards are visible to other ETASheet users. Scores made for copyrighted songs are visible only to the person who asked and the people who wanted it. Reports are private.

Children

ETASheet is for music learners of all ages, used with a parent's or teacher's permission for young children. Please do not share personal information (phone numbers, addresses, school names) in the community.

Deleting your account

In the app: Account > Delete account (also in the web version at this site after signing in). This deletes your profile, credits, plan link, notifications and sign-ins. Your posts remain, shown as "Deleted user", so conversations still make sense; ask us to remove them too through Account > Report a problem. Cancel any subscription in Google Play as well.

Where data is kept

On our server at Fly.io (Singapore region), with backups. Data is kept while your account exists.

Contact

Use Account > Report a problem or idea in the app, or the contact email on the Google Play listing.
