Haptune privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026. Haptune is made by sloky. 中文 · Bahasa Melayu

The short version

Your music library, recordings and practice history stay on your device. We keep only what the community and your plan need: your profile, what you post, your credits and your practice minutes, plus usage statistics you can switch off. We never sell data, there are no ads, and no advertising or tracking companies receive anything.

What stays on your device

  • Your pieces (scores, chord sheets, PDFs and photos you import) and your practice history.
  • The microphone: Play Along and the tuner listen on your device. A Play Along take is recorded on your device so you can listen back; it is sent only if you choose to send it to your teacher in a class, or post it on Stage.

What the Haptune server keeps

  • Your account: a display name, username, avatar colour, instruments, level and short bio you choose; a password if you set one (stored only as a secure hash); the devices you signed in on. If you sign in with Google: your Google account ID and email address (the email is shown only to you), and your first name if you have not chosen one.
  • Google connections (only if you turn them on): Calendar (to add class sessions with a Meet link), Drive (to save files you export) and Contacts (to pick people to invite). Each asks for its own permission when you first use it; we keep an encrypted key to act on that permission and nothing else from your Google account. Remove any of them in Settings > Connections or in your Google account; if you say no, the feature still works without Google (a calendar file, a download, typing the invite yourself).
  • Google user data: Haptune uses data from Google only to provide the feature you turned on (sign-in, class sessions in your Calendar, files you save to Drive, people you pick from Contacts). It is not sold, not used for ads, not used to train AI models, and not shown to other people. Haptune's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Community: requests, questions, answers, comments, scores and attachments you post; claps, follows, blocks and reports; notifications.
  • Credits and plan: your credit history, your plan and how many AI features you used this month.
  • Practice minutes per day, for streaks and leaderboards.
  • Classes: if you join a class, your results (scores, the bars and notes that went wrong, practice time) and the recordings you choose to send. Only the class teacher (and you) can see and listen to them. Open classes show their name, description, teacher's name and number of students to everyone.
  • Problem reports: what you write, and technical details (app version, device type, recent errors) only if you leave "Include technical details" on.

Usage statistics

To see what helps people practise and what doesn't, the app sends us short usage events: when it opens and closes, which screen is shown and for how long, when a piece is opened, Play Along started and finished (with the score), an export, a post, joining a class, a Sound shared, a sound check result, a question to Hapi, and error messages with the device type. They go only to our own server; no analytics or advertising company is involved.

  • Not collected: recordings, the contents of your sheets, what you type, your contacts, or your IP address. Your country is kept as two letters (from your network or your device's language setting).
  • How long: individual events are deleted after 180 days; after that only daily totals remain (how many people used a feature on a day), which do not identify anyone.
  • Your choice: turn it off in Settings > Privacy and security > Share usage statistics. The app then sends no events at all.
  • Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it, balanced by the small, minimised data above and your ability to switch it off. Optional Google permissions are used only with your consent.

Who at Haptune can see your data

The owner of Haptune, and developers the owner chooses, can open a dashboard that shows totals (how many people use each feature) and, if they are allowed to manage accounts, one account's details and activity, to answer support requests, fix problems, or act on reports. Access is limited by role, needs a second sign-in step every time, and every look at one person's details or action on an account is written to a log that cannot be changed.

AI features

When you use Plus or Pro features (reading music from photos or PDFs, finding songs online, the Hapi tutor), the page, photo, search or question you send is processed by Anthropic's Claude API to produce the answer. It is not used to train AI models. Free features do not use AI.

Purchases

Subscriptions are bought through Google Play (and later the App Store). Payments are handled by the store; we never see your card. RevenueCat tells our server which plan your account has.

Who can see what

Your profile, public posts and leaderboards are visible to other Haptune users. Scores made for copyrighted songs are visible only to the person who asked and the people who wanted it. Reports are private.

Children

Haptune is for music learners of all ages, used with a parent's or teacher's permission for young children. Please do not share personal information (phone numbers, addresses, school names) in the community.

Your rights: see, download, correct, delete

Under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act and, for people in Europe, the GDPR, you can see and download what we keep (Settings > Privacy and security > Download my data, a file you can open), correct your profile in the app, withdraw consent for Google connections, switch off usage statistics, and delete your account. For anything else, contact us (below); we answer within 21 days.

Deleting your account

In the app: Account > Delete account (also in the web version at this site after signing in). This deletes your profile, credits, plan link, notifications, sign-ins and usage events. Your posts remain, shown as "Deleted user", so conversations still make sense; ask us to remove them too through Account > Report a problem. Cancel any subscription in Google Play as well.

Where data is kept

On our server at Fly.io (Singapore region), with backups. Data is kept while your account exists.

Contact

Use Account > Report a problem or idea in the app, or the contact email on the Google Play listing.

中文摘要

使用统计：App 会把简短的使用记录发送到我们自己的服务器：打开和关闭 App、显示哪个页面及停留时长、打开曲目、开始和完成跟弹（含分数）、导出、发帖、加入班级、分享声音作品、声音检测结果、向 Hapi 提问，以及错误信息和设备类型。不会发给任何分析或广告公司。

不收集：录音、乐谱内容、你输入的文字、联系人或 IP 地址。国家/地区只保存两个字母。保存期限：单条记录 180 天后删除，之后只保留不识别个人的每日总数。你的选择：在“设置 › 隐私与安全 › 分享使用统计”中关闭后，App 不再发送任何记录。

谁能查看：Haptune 的所有者以及其指定的开发者可以查看统计总数；获准管理账号的人员可查看单个账号的资料和动态，用于客服、修复问题或处理举报。每次查看都需要两步验证，所有查看和操作都会记入无法修改的日志。

你的权利（马来西亚《个人数据保护法》及 GDPR）：你可以在“设置 › 隐私与安全 › 下载我的数据”下载我们保存的全部资料、在 App 中更正资料、撤回 Google 连接授权、关闭使用统计并删除账号（删除时一并删除使用记录）。Google 日历、云端硬盘和联系人仅在你使用相关功能并同意时才连接。

Ringkasan Bahasa Melayu

Statistik penggunaan: aplikasi menghantar rekod ringkas ke pelayan kami sendiri: bila aplikasi dibuka dan ditutup, skrin yang dipaparkan dan berapa lama, karya dibuka, Play Along dimulakan dan diselesaikan (dengan skor), eksport, siaran, menyertai kelas, Bunyi dikongsi, keputusan semakan bunyi, soalan kepada Hapi, dan mesej ralat dengan jenis peranti. Tiada syarikat analitik atau iklan menerimanya.

Tidak dikumpul: rakaman, kandungan skor, apa yang anda taip, kenalan atau alamat IP. Negara disimpan sebagai dua huruf sahaja. Tempoh simpanan: rekod individu dipadam selepas 180 hari; selepas itu hanya jumlah harian yang tidak mengenal pasti sesiapa. Pilihan anda: matikan di Tetapan › Privasi dan keselamatan › Kongsi statistik penggunaan; aplikasi tidak lagi menghantar apa-apa rekod.

Siapa boleh melihat: pemilik Haptune dan pembangun yang dipilihnya boleh melihat jumlah keseluruhan; mereka yang dibenarkan mengurus akaun boleh melihat butiran dan aktiviti satu akaun untuk sokongan, membaiki masalah atau menangani laporan. Akses memerlukan pengesahan 2 langkah setiap kali, dan setiap paparan atau tindakan direkodkan dalam log yang tidak boleh diubah.

Hak anda (Akta Perlindungan Data Peribadi Malaysia dan GDPR): muat turun semua data kami tentang anda di Tetapan › Privasi dan keselamatan › Muat turun data saya, betulkan profil dalam aplikasi, tarik balik kebenaran sambungan Google, matikan statistik penggunaan dan padam akaun (rekod penggunaan turut dipadam). Google Kalendar, Drive dan Kenalan hanya disambungkan apabila anda menggunakan ciri itu dan bersetuju.
