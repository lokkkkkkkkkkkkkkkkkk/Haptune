Refund policy

Last updated 12 November 2026.

Card payments on the website (Stripe)

If Melovyn Plus or Pro is not for you, ask within 7 days of your first payment and we refund it in full. After that, cancel any time and your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for; we do not refund part of a period. If you were charged by mistake (for example twice), tell us and we put it right.

DuitNow QR and bank transfer (Malaysia)

The same 7 days apply. Refunds go back to the bank account the payment came from.

Google Play

Purchases made in the Android app are handled by Google. Ask for a refund in Google Play (Account → Payments and subscriptions → Budget and history), under Google's refund rules.

Teacher verification fee

Refunded in full if your profile is not approved. Once a profile has been verified and listed, the fee is not refunded.

How to ask

In the app: Me → Send feedback → Billing. Tell us the name on your Melovyn account and the date of the payment. We answer within three working days.
